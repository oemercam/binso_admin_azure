# Mobile/PWA list content standard — V34

Mobile/PWA overview lists intentionally show less information than desktop.

Each row contains:
1. primary identity/title
2. at most one short context line
3. disclosure chevron

Not shown in Mobile/PWA overview rows:
- status
- validity/due dates
- amount
- billing interval
- budget/used/rest values
- margin
- utilisation
- other operational metrics

Current context line by page:
- Kunden: contact person or city fallback
- Mitarbeitende: role
- Verträge: customer
- Angebote: customer
- Aufträge: customer
- Rechnungen: order or period

The removed information remains available in the detail/preview and on desktop.
