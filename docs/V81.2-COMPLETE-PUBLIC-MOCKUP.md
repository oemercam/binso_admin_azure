V81.2 applies the approved Binso One public mockup language across the complete public and pre-app surface while leaving the authenticated customer application under `app/(app)` visually unchanged.

## Design rules

- compact desktop header with Binso One brand, centred product navigation, customer/admin access and trial CTA
- same 1180 px content system and compact SaaS proportions as the approved mockup
- strong but restrained typography, black/white base and one blue action colour
- real Binso One product screenshots only where product visuals are used
- screenshots always contained, never full-bleed, and kept compact
- alternating content/product rows on product pages
- consistent compact cards, forms, FAQ rows, status surfaces and legal typography
- fullscreen mobile menu and stacked mobile sections from 320–430 px
- footer, spacing and safe-area handling shared on every public page
- authenticated customer dashboard and `app/(app)` presentation unchanged
