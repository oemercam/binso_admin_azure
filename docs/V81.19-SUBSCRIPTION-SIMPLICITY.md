# V81.19 – Subscription Simplicity

Binso One keeps exactly three self-service plans: Starter, Business and Professional. Enterprise remains an individually managed contract based on Professional and is not exposed as a fourth self-service option.

## Product rules

- Navigation and quick actions are entitlement-driven. Unavailable modules are not shown as locked global navigation items.
- Starter focuses on the core flow: customers, quotes, orders, invoices and export.
- Business adds team workflows: time, contracts, finance, employees, expenses, reminders, approvals and import.
- Professional adds advanced control: accounting, margin, audit, API and automations.
- Direct URLs remain protected by EntitlementGate and server-side permission/entitlement checks.
- The organization subscription sheet only offers the three self-service plans.
- Enterprise remains manageable by the Binso platform/operator path, not customer self-service.
- Settings hide automation controls when the current plan contains neither reminders nor automations.
- Data remains reachable for Starter export-only access.

## Local environment safety

Full-project replacement must preserve `.env`, `.env.local`, `.env.development.local`, `.env.production.local` and `.env.example`, and must never overwrite local certificate/key files.
