# V70.5 — Spacious landing sections

V70.5 keeps the approved hero and restructures the public landing page below it.

## Principles

- Landing page stays fixed light: white canvas, black primary typography.
- The internal authenticated application is not changed.
- Fewer generic cards below the hero.
- Large product visuals and alternating text/visual sections replace the former feature-card grid.
- Business flow is a continuous visual timeline rather than a row of boxed cards.
- Onboarding is a simple four-step sequence.
- Security/trust is presented as calm columns with dividers instead of cards.
- Pricing cards remain because card comparison is appropriate for plans.
- FAQ remains a simple accordion list.

## Technical safeguards

- Original Binso logo continues through the canonical `BinsoLogo` component.
- Decorative mockup controls are non-interactive elements, not fake buttons.
- Cookie settings use the canonical shared `CloseButton` control.
- Version: 0.70.5.
