# V81.7 Calmer Visual Rhythm

V81.7 reduces repetitive screenshot-heavy sections on the public landing page.

- Hero keeps the primary laptop product view.
- Project management keeps a real product screenshot.
- Time tracking now uses a compact KPI/timer composition instead of another screenshot.
- Billing keeps the next real product screenshot, creating visual breathing room between image-heavy sections.
- Integrations remain diagrammatic, followed by references, testimonials and pricing CTA.
- Mobile keeps copy first and the calmer visual second.
- Authenticated app visuals remain unchanged.
