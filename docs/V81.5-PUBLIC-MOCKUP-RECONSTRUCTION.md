# V81.5 Public Mockup Reconstruction

V81.5 rebuilds the public landing composition from the approved Binso One reference rather than incrementally restyling the previous layout.

- full-width soft hero band with fixed inner grid and laptop presentation;
- compact alternating product rows with screenshot/text proportions matching the reference;
- integrations, proof, testimonials and price CTA rebuilt as one compact lower stack;
- mobile is copy-first, screenshot-second with full-width CTAs and consistent 320–430 px spacing;
- public detail, auth and legal pages inherit the same max-width, typography, spacing and card treatment;
- authenticated `app/(app)` presentation is intentionally untouched;
- customer names/logos in the reference are not fabricated; neutral placeholders remain until approved real references exist.
